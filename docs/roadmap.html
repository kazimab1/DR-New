The result that matters most so far

M1 reaches grade-1 F1 0.142; M2a segments microaneurysms at Dice 0.316. Grade 1 is microaneurysms, so the evidence pathway sees the thing the grader is blind to. That asymmetry is the mechanism the whole thesis rests on, and it is now measured rather than assumed.

  • Evidence pathway — Stage C

    Weeks 6–9 · 0.9 GPU-h
    done

    C1 FAIL at 0.686 DD. Two attempts: the coordinate head flips the disc's side on 10 of 83 images, carrying 47% of the error; the heatmap head built to fix that gave 27 of 83 and worse error everywhere. The remedy is refuted, the cause is not established, M3 runs count-only.

    C2 on DDR: mean Dice 0.505, MA 0.344. Over-segments rare lesions — soft exudate fires on 51 of 106 negatives.

    C3 transfers: 0.425 on held-out IDRiD against 0.505 in-domain, −16% relative. Not the collapse the experiment was built to detect, and the first evidence for H1′.

    C4 QWK 0.375 against M1's 0.679 on 11 767 images the segmenter never saw. Informative but weaker — the premise holds. Read it as a floor: only 3 of 5 grades are reachable, and R3* carries 35% of the corpus.

Waiting on you

Two settled by Stage C, two still open

  • 1 · Does M2b get a second attempt? — settled: no

    C1 fails at 0.686 DD, but 12% of images are outright laterality flips and the other 88% sit at 0.333 DD. A heatmap head can represent both possible disc positions and let the evidence pick; a regressed coordinate must commit to one. Retraining costs 1.3 minutes.

    It was tried and it failed: 27 of 83 flips against 10, and worse on the fovea where bimodality cannot apply. A third attempt (weighted heatmap loss, 1.3 min) is declined — landmark accuracy is not the contribution.

  • 2 · Who builds M3, and when? — settled: built

    Nothing in Phase 4 builds the reasoner, yet C4 runs on it and Phase 4's exit condition requires C4. It is also what turns lesion masks into the structured facts the 4-2-1 rule reads. This is the largest unscheduled piece of work in the project.

    Built and run. M3 is deterministic Python, no learned parameters, five ICDR rules, and C4 measured it at QWK 0.375. Phase 4 closed properly.

  • 3 · How is Phase 6 trimmed to budget?

    The frozen recipe projects to 21.7 GPU-hours against a ~20 h plan, on 59 842 real training rows. Kaggle allows 30 h per week, so it fits in a week — but Phase 7 needs another ~10, and the two together do not.

    trim seeds 3 → 2  ·  trim epochs 10 → 8  ·  split across two weeks — whichever, it is a pre-registration entry, not a mid-run improvisation

  • 4 · Does C2 get its +IDRiD arm back?

    C2 trains on DDR with IDRiD held out, which buys a genuine external domain for C3 at the cost of the +IDRiD training arm the register specifies. IDRiD contributes ~81 annotated images.

    keep held out (current) — external validity for the transfer claim  ·  train on both — marginally better segmenter, no cross-domain test left

For the freeze

Deviations that must be written down in Phase 5

  1. C1 fails its gate

    0.686 DD against a pre-specified 0.5. Record the failure, the laterality diagnosis, and whichever fallback M3 ends up using. The median (0.300 DD) is a diagnostic and must never be substituted for the criterion.

  2. C2's +IDRiD arm did not run

    IDRiD is held out of M2a training to serve as C3's external domain. A deliberate trade, and a departure from the register as written.

  3. B4 tested two samplers, not three

    class_balanced and stratified_exposure proved identical at default epoch_samples — same weights, same draw count, same index sequence. The register says three arms; two ran.

  4. B1 cannot test what motivated it

    The 384/512/768 sweep reads one 512 px cache, so the 768 arm upsamples. Report B1 as choosing an operating point under a fixed cache, not as a resolution-sensitivity study.

  5. EyePACS is 693 images short, non-randomly

    χ² = 105.6 on 4 df; grade 4 lost at three times grade 0's rate. Upstream of this project, prevalence impact negligible (rDR 19.34% → 19.23%), but a severity-dependent selection effect belongs in the limitations of a referral study.
